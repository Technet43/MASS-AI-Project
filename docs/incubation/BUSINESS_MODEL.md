# MASS-AI — Business Model

## Status of this document

This document describes a commercial design to validate during pilot discovery. It is a **pricing hypothesis**, not a quote, contract, customer commitment, pipeline, revenue record, savings estimate, or forecast.

No verified customer pricing discussion, paid pilot, or revenue is documented in this repository.

## Customer problem to validate

Potential utility partners may need a more efficient way to prioritize investigations. The project must validate:

- whether the workflow reduces analyst effort or improves the quality of a review queue;
- which deployment and data-governance model a partner can accept;
- what evidence and accountability are needed before a partner pays; and
- whether a commercial outcome-based component is appropriate and lawful.

## Pricing hypothesis

A possible hybrid structure has three components:

1. **Pilot and onboarding scope**

   Data mapping, agreed evaluation design, setup, and partner-specific support.

2. **Recurring platform access**

   Access to the approved workflow, reporting, support, and integration maintenance.

3. **Result-based component**

   Consider only after the partner agrees on attribution, verification, auditability, legal terms, and a baseline.

All monetary values, payment percentages, customer counts, revenue scenarios, savings estimates, and unit-economics calculations from earlier drafts remain unvalidated pricing hypotheses. They should not be presented as market facts or financial projections without sourced assumptions and partner input.

## Pilot economics to learn

The first pilot should seek evidence, not an unsupported revenue claim. Questions to answer include:

- What implementation work is required to handle the partner’s actual data?
- What review capacity and support level are required?
- Which outcomes can be measured fairly?
- What costs belong to the partner, the project, and any integration provider?
- Which commercial terms would make a follow-on deployment viable?

## Commercial validation gates

Before publishing a price list or revenue plan:

- conduct and document customer discovery;
- agree a narrow pilot scope and data-governance model;
- define measurable outcomes and attribution rules;
- document the legal and procurement constraints; and
- separate measured results from assumptions in every presentation.

## Incubation message

“We have a commercial hypothesis: a scoped pilot, recurring platform access, and a possible result-based component. We have not yet validated prices, revenue, or customer willingness to pay. The first pilot is designed to produce that evidence.”
